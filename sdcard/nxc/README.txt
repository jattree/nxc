.nxc patches go here, one folder per game.

Copy a game's folder from the nxc repository's patches/ folder into this one,
then put your own copy of the game in that folder, beside the .nxc, named
exactly as the .nfo says and with the MD5 it gives. For example:

  /nxc/Jet Pac (1983)(Ultimate Play The Game)(16K)/
      Jet Pac (1983)(Ultimate Play The Game)(16K)[a2][a2].nxc
      Jet Pac (1983)(Ultimate Play The Game)(16K)[a2][a2].nfo
      Jet Pac (1983)(Ultimate Play The Game)(16K)[a2][a2].z80   <- yours

Then choose the .nxc in the Browser.

https://github.com/jattree/nxc
